Overview

OneAssist, a prominent Indian insurtech company, has been a trusted partner in safeguarding gadgets, home appliances, and wallets since its inception in 2011.

Recently, we launched “Quick Repairs,” an on-demand home service across India, allowing users to conveniently book repair services via the app or website.

Despite a promising start, data analysis revealed a significant drop-off in the conversion funnel, prompting an in-depth examination of the user experience (UX) to identify and address underlying issues.

Problem statement

Quick Repairs service, designed for easy on-demand appliance and gadget repairs, is experiencing a higher than expected rate of abandoned payments. Data shows customers are encountering friction points during the checkout process, hindering successful service bookings.

This case study aims to identify the usability issues in the payment flow that are causing customers to abandon their bookings and propose solutions to improve the completion rate.

Goal and objective

Outcome-oriented: Increase the completion rate of “Quick Repairs” service bookings through improved payment flow design.

Action-oriented: Identify and address usability issues within the booking flow that are causing customer drop-off.

Exercise 1

Obtaining insights directly from customers

We defined a segment and recruited users to conduct interviews to gather as many insights and qualitative feedback as possible. We spoke to more than 100 such customers and uncovered several key insights.

Key pain points
  • Reluctance to pay upfront for services.
  • Lack of trust in technician visits and service quality.
  • Uncertainty regarding repair costs and appliance worthiness.
  • Inconvenient time slot availability.
Solutions proposed
  • By offering transparent pricing and a ‘Pay Later’ option, OneAssist can alleviate customer’s concerns about upfront payment and uncertain costs.
  • Highlighting positive customer testimonials and technician expertise can enhance user trust in the Quick Repairs service.

Thanks to the product managers for steering the feedback in the right direction. As the data suggested, we swiftly introduced ‘Pay Later’ for the first time.

Customers can now book their service for free and pay later after the service, at their convenience.

Exercise 2

Redesigning the category page and conducting A/B testing

Next, we focused on the initial step of the funnel, where a significant drop-off occurred. The category page serves as the customer’s first encounter with the service, showcasing its promises, features, and benefits.

Despite serving over 20 lakh customers across India, first-time visitors were struggling to trust our service.

As a solution, we decided to redesign the page with one goal in mind: to let our existing customers speak about the brand and the service.

  • Highlighting positive customer testimonials and technician expertise can enhance customer’s trust in the Quick Repairs service.
  • Emphasizing the expertise of OneAssist’s certified technicians and showcasing positive customer feedback can enhance customer’s confidence in the service’s reliability and quality.

Points covered in our new design

  • Video Testimonial: Featuring customer testimonials to build trust in the brand and service.
  • Customer Feedback and Ratings: Displaying recent feedback and ratings from customers.
  • OneAssist Certified Technicians: Highlighting the expertise of our certified technicians and how they differ from local mechanics.
  • Covered Appliance Brands: Listing popular appliance brands that we service.
Result

The number of users proceeding from the category page increased by 53%. This indirectly contributed to further improvement in our overall funnel performance.

Exercise 3

Addressing usability issues and optimizing UI

Previously, the second step in the journey involved adding the number of appliances and selecting the type of service or repair.

Consistent 50% drop-off from this page

Possible reasons (assumptions)

  • The “Proceed to Book + Pay After Service” button is hard to discover.
  • If discovered, it is in a disabled state until an appliance is added.
  • Price is introduced for the first time, causing users to drop off to compare.
  • The chat support icon interferes with the “Proceed to Book” button, creating friction.

Solutions

  • Make the primary Call-to-Action (CTA) visible on the first fold for easy discovery.
  • Enable the button and guide users to add appliances and proceed.
  • Shift the chat icon slightly upwards to reduce interference.
Result

The funnel from this step improved from 75.2% to 78.1%.

Exercise 4

Optimizing task flow

We analyzed and documented the current flow from the user’s perspective and identified a 45% drop in the login flow.

To reduce the drop and increase conversion, we planned to test a new journey with just 1 appliance. After adding the appliance, users can choose a slot, enter details, and then verify the number.

Result
  • The drop from the “Proceed to Book” step to the next step reduced from 25.4% to 0.27%.
  • Overall users reaching the last step increased from 4.94% to 11.03%.

Conclusion and learnings

The implementation of these UX enhancements yielded remarkable improvements in the Quick Repairs service conversion metrics:

  • Conversion rate increased by an impressive 5x, reflecting the success of the optimization efforts.
  • Significant enhancements in user journey completion rates and overall funnel performance.
  • Enhanced trust, transparency, and convenience resulted in a more seamless booking experience for users, fostering greater satisfaction and loyalty.

UX optimization is an ongoing process. There is always room for improvement. Through these exercises, we’ve learned that even small enhancements can yield significant results. We have many more feature improvements planned for the upcoming days.
